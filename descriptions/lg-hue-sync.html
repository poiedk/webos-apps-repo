LG Hue Sync

Native ambient-light synchronization for rooted LG webOS TVs.

LG Hue Sync captures the displayed image directly on the TV and streams matching colours to WLED, Philips Hue Entertainment and Nanoleaf devices on the local network.

  • WLED realtime DDP streaming with per-LED perimeter sampling
  • Philips Hue Entertainment streaming
  • Nanoleaf 4D support
  • Legacy webOS 3.x ARMv7 build for older LG TVs
  • Automatic Homebrew startup hook and persistent configuration
  • LAN dashboard for status, calibration and output controls

Requires a rooted LG webOS TV with Homebrew Channel. Capture support depends on the private capture APIs available on the TV firmware.
